Is the quote real, and does it say that?

VeriQuote makes a model attach a verbatim quote to every cited claim, then checks each citation twice: a deterministic matcher asks whether the quote is really in the source, and an LLM judge asks whether the quote supports the claim. Each check is blind exactly where the other one sees. Pick an example or write your own.
