VeriQuote · open-source library and CLI

Is the quote in the source, and does it support the claim?

Language models put citations like [1] behind their claims, and checking one means opening the source and finding the passage. A recent study on clinical guideline questions found that a frontier model quoted its sources word for word for 98% of its claims, yet only 37% of the claims were fully supported by those quotes.

VeriQuote makes the model attach a verbatim quote to every citation and checks each quote twice. A deterministic matcher looks for the quote in the source. An LLM judge reads the quote next to the claim and decides whether it supports it. A citation passes only if both let it through.
