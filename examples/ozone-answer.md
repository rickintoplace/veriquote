The ozone layer absorbs 97 to 99 percent of the Sun's medium-frequency ultraviolet light.[1]{c1}
It was discovered in 1913 by the British meteorologist G. M. B. Dobson.[1]{c2}
Under the Montreal Protocol, all CFC production was banned immediately in 1987.[1]{c3}
The treaty limited CFC production to the levels of 1986.[1]{c4}
Ozone depletion has since been fully reversed in every region of the atmosphere.
